# Request Data Deletion — BugarAI

Last updated: October 6, 2026

## What is data deletion?

Data deletion means removing your fitness, health, and activity data from BugarAI while keeping your account active so you can continue using the app.

This is different from **account deletion**, which permanently removes your account and all data.

## Data deletion options

### Option 1: Delete data inside the app

Currently, the fastest way to remove your data is to delete your account from the app:

1. Open the **BugarAI** app
2. Go to **Profile**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm the deletion

This will delete your account and all associated data.

### Option 2: Request data deletion by email

If you want to keep your account but delete your fitness and health data, email us:

**Email:** [azeinn@gmail.com](mailto:azeinn@gmail.com?subject=BugarAI%20Data%20Deletion%20Request)

Please include:

- The email address associated with your BugarAI account
- A clear statement that you want your health/fitness data deleted
- Optionally, specify which data you want deleted (e.g., workouts, nutrition logs, chat history, weight logs, or everything)

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm via email.

## What data can be deleted

You can request deletion of:

- Workout logs and exercise history
- Nutrition logs and meal history
- Weight logs and body measurements
- AI chat history
- Step and activity data synced to our servers
- Fitness goals and preferences
- Profile photo and profile information

## What data is kept

If you request data deletion without account deletion, we will keep only the minimum information required to maintain your account:

- Your user ID
- Your email address
- Your name

This allows you to continue logging in.

## What data is not deleted

Some data may be retained for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes, or as required by law:

- Purchase records required for tax and accounting purposes
- Anonymous, aggregated analytics data that cannot identify you

## Third-party data

BugarAI uses third-party services such as Google Firebase, RevenueCat, Google AdMob, and Health Connect. Deleting data from our servers does not delete data stored locally on your device or data managed directly by these services. You may need to manage your data with those services separately.
