# Request Account Deletion — BugarAI

Last updated: October 6, 2026

## How to delete your account

The fastest way to delete your BugarAI account and all associated data is directly inside the app:

1. Open the **BugarAI** app
2. Go to **Profile**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm the deletion

Once confirmed, your account and all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems.

## Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us:

**Email:** [azeinn@gmail.com](mailto:azeinn@gmail.com?subject=BugarAI%20Account%20Deletion%20Request)

Please include the following information in your request:

- The email address associated with your BugarAI account
- A clear statement that you want your account and data deleted

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion via email.

## What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

- Account information (name, email, profile photo)
- Fitness and health data (workouts, nutrition logs, weight logs, body measurements)
- Step and activity data synced to our servers
- AI chat history
- Goals and preferences
- Subscription tier information stored in our database

## What data is not deleted

Some data may be retained for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes, or as required by law:

- Purchase records required for tax and accounting purposes (retained as long as legally required)
- Anonymous, aggregated analytics data that cannot identify you

## Third-party data

BugarAI uses the following third-party services. Deleting your BugarAI account removes data from our servers, but you may also want to manage your data directly with these services:

- **Google / Firebase**: Used for authentication and cloud data storage
- **RevenueCat**: Used for subscription management
- **Google AdMob**: Used for advertising in the free version
- **Health Connect**: Health data stored on your device remains under your control
