# BugarAI Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 6, 2026

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BugarAI ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the BugarAI mobile application (the "App"). BugarAI is an AI-powered fitness companion that helps you track workouts, nutrition, steps, and receive AI coaching.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and Profile Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address**
- **Name** (or display name)
- **Profile photo** (optional, uploaded by you)
- **User ID** (a unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication)
- **Sign-in method** (email/password or Google Sign-In)

### 1.2 Health and Fitness Information

BugarAI is a fitness app. With your permission, we collect and store:

- **Body measurements**: weight, height, age, gender
- **Fitness goals**: calories, protein, carbs, fats, water, and step goals
- **Workout data**: exercises performed, sets, reps, weights, duration, timestamps
- **Nutrition data**: meals logged, food items, calories, macronutrients
- **Step and activity data**: daily steps, estimated calories burned, distance, active minutes
- **Body measurement logs**: chest, waist, hips, arms, and other measurements you choose to track

This data is collected from your direct input, device sensors (pedometer), and optionally from **Health Connect** on your Android device.

### 1.3 Photos

- **Meal photos**: If you use the AI Meal Scanner, you may take or select a photo of your food. The image is sent to our secure Cloud Function and analyzed by Google's Gemini AI. The image is used solely to estimate nutritional information and is not stored permanently by us.
- **Profile photos**: If you upload a profile picture, it is stored in your user record.

### 1.4 AI Chat and Generated Content

When you use the AI Coach, your messages and AI responses may be saved to provide you with chat history. These messages may include fitness-related questions and personalized coaching responses.

### 1.5 Usage and Device Information

We automatically collect certain technical and usage information:

- **Device type and operating system version**
- **App interactions** and feature usage
- **Advertising ID** (for personalized advertising via Google AdMob)
- **Device identifiers** used by Firebase and RevenueCat for authentication and subscription management
- **In-app purchase and subscription history** (managed by RevenueCat and Google Play Billing)
- **Crash and error logs** (only basic error messages visible in the app; we do not use a third-party crash reporting service)

### 1.6 Information from Third Parties

- **Google Sign-In**: If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google, subject to your Google privacy settings.
- **Health Connect**: With your explicit permission, we may read step and activity data from Health Connect on your device.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Provide the core fitness tracking features of the App
- Calculate personalized nutrition and workout recommendations
- Power the AI Coach and Meal Scanner features
- Sync your data across devices
- Process in-app purchases and subscriptions
- Show advertisements to free-tier users
- Send optional reminder notifications
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Maintain security and prevent fraud

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers necessary to operate the App:

### 3.1 Google Firebase

We use Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage) to host and secure your data. Your data is stored in Firebase/Firestore, which is protected by Google's security infrastructure.

### 3.2 Google AdMob

We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect your advertising ID and device information to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

### 3.3 RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat processes your purchase history and device identifiers to verify entitlements.

### 3.4 Google Gemini API

When you use the AI Meal Scanner or AI Coach, your messages and meal photos are sent through our Cloud Function to Google's Gemini API for processing. We do not permanently store meal photos. Chat messages may be stored to maintain your chat history.

### 3.5 Health Connect

Health Connect is an on-device health platform. When you connect Health Connect, step and activity data is read from and optionally written to your device's Health Connect datastore. This data is not shared with third parties beyond what is necessary to display it in the App.

### 3.6 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage

The App stores data locally on your device using **Hive** encrypted databases. The local data is encrypted with AES-256 encryption, and the encryption key is stored securely using Android Keystore / iOS Keychain via `flutter_secure_storage`.

### 4.2 Cloud Storage

Your account data, workout logs, nutrition logs, and chat history are stored in **Firebase Firestore**. Google Cloud Platform encrypts data at rest and in transit by default.

### 4.3 Data Transmission

All data transmitted between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

### 4.4 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

- Input sanitization and validation
- Brute-force protection for login attempts
- Secure authentication via Firebase Auth
- Encrypted local storage
- HTTPS for all network communication

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information.

## 5. Health Connect Disclosure

BugarAI integrates with **Health Connect**, Google's on-device health platform.

### Permissions We Request

With your explicit consent, we may access the following Health Connect data types:

- **Steps** (read and write)
- **Total calories burned** (read)
- **Active calories burned** (read)
- **Distance** (read)
- **Exercise sessions** (read)

### How We Use Health Connect Data

- To display your daily step count and activity summary
- To estimate calories burned based on your activity
- To merge step data from multiple sources for accuracy
- To provide progress insights and coaching

### Your Control

You can connect or disconnect Health Connect at any time from the **Profile** screen in the App. You can also revoke permissions from your device's Health Connect settings.

### Data Retention

Health data synced to Firestore is retained until you delete your account. Local Health Connect data remains under your control on your device.

## 6. Advertising

The free version of BugarAI displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can:

- Upgrade to a paid subscription (Lite or Pro) to remove ads
- Opt out of personalized ads through your Android device settings: **Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization**

We do not share your health or fitness data with AdMob.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and associated data
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Withdraw consent for optional features (Health Connect, camera, notifications)

### Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly in the App:

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Select **Delete Account**
3. Confirm the deletion

When you delete your account, we remove your Firestore user document and Firebase Authentication record. Some data may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period but will not be used.

If you cannot access the App, you can request account deletion by contacting us at the email address below.
